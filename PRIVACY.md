# Tonnage privacy policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

Tonnage is a strength-training app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This page explains what it does with your data.

## What stays on your device

Your workouts, sets, programs, body measurements, photos and profile are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch. If iCloud is on, Apple syncs them to your private iCloud account. We never see them.

## Apple Health

With your permission, Tonnage reads bodyweight, sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, temperature, breathing rate, workouts and nutrition from Apple Health. It writes your workouts and bodyweight back. Health data stays on your device. It's never sold, shared or used for advertising.

## Oura

If you connect your Oura account, Tonnage reads your daily readiness, sleep and heart-rate data from Oura to show your readiness and guide your training. Your Oura data goes directly from Oura to your phone. Tonnage's server only helps with signing in to Oura and never stores or sees your Oura data. You can disconnect anytime in Settings or in your Oura account.

## AI Coach

When you use the AI Coach, the request (your question plus a summary of your training, profile and recovery) is sent to Anthropic to generate a reply. Without your own API key, it goes through Tonnage's server, which forwards it and counts daily usage by network address. It doesn't store the content. Anthropic's privacy policy covers how it handles requests.

## What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing your data.
