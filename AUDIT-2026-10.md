## Verdict

**The training math is sound.** Tonnage, top sets, the estimated one-rep max, PR detection, the RPE progression rules, deloads, layoff scaling, plate math for normal loads and warm-up ramps all do what `TESTING_PLAYBOOK.md` says. On Week 1 of a new block, the app correctly carries forward the heaviest top set from the previous block, not the deload weight.

**What isn't release-ready:** the build itself, watch sync, the proxy's abuse protection, and handling of AI-written plans.

## Calculation edge cases (findings only)

- **Fixed** (`6c78028`): barbell warm-ups, deloads, layoffs and drop sets floor at the empty bar ("empty bar × 10"). — **Warm-ups and deloads can go below the empty bar.** Warm-ups are percent-based with no equipment floor: a 95 lb overhead press warms up with 40 lb, and a 65 lb bench deloads to 40 lb. Any barbell working weight under ~112 lb gets a first warm-up under 45. Fix: use "Empty bar × 10" as the floor for barbell lifts.
- **Fixed** (`6c78028`): 2.5 lb for isolation lifts, next dumbbell (2.5 lb steps to 25, then 5) for dumbbells, via a name-inferred `LoadProfile`. — **Flat +5 lb jumps are large for small lifts.** A 15 lb lateral raise becomes 20 lb (+33%). Fix: use smaller or percentage steps for isolation and dumbbell lifts (2.5 lb, or the next dumbbell).
- **Fixed on the phone** (`6c78028`): planks, hangs and wall sits log time and get no load advice. The watch still logs them as weight × reps (**Deferred**: needs a timed logger on the watch). — **Planks have no time field.** They're treated as strength (weight, reps, RPE), so the coach can say "add 5 lb" to a plank.
- **Deferred**: not in the fix list, and treating singles as exact changes which sets count as PRs, which is a product call. — **A true single reads as a 3% higher e1RM.** Epley gives 300 × 1 → 310. Many apps treat a 1-rep set as exact. Display only; PR firing is unaffected.
- **Fixed** (`6c78028`): PR history, PR toasts and last-week references key on the canonical name. — **PR history splits on spelling.** History is keyed by exact exercise name, so "Bench Press" and "bench press" track separately. Run names through `ExerciseCanonicalizer`.

## Proxy harness: 37/37

`proxy.test.mjs` passes 37 of 37. Fixture update: the env carries an `APP_TOKEN` and every request sends `x-app-token`; a header given as `undefined` is omitted. Three expectations changed from 200 to 429 (with no upstream call): a missing, 300-character or `:`-containing `x-device-id` no longer buys a fresh allowance. The quota now belongs to the network (IPv4 / IPv6 /64), not the client-chosen header, which is what the "rotated x-device-id" check requires. `quota-do.test.mjs` covers the Durable Object path (8/8).

## Product suggestions

1. **Close the watch loop.** A reps-left picker on the watch and synced programs turn the watch from a logger into the place people actually train. That's the feature most likely to set Tonnage apart.
2. **Make progression equipment-aware.** Tell the engine whether a lift is a barbell, dumbbell, machine or bodyweight lift. It fixes items 13–15 and the warm-up and deload floors in one change, and the advice starts to feel like a real coach.
3. **Pay for the AI with a subscription.** A shared key with a free daily allowance is an open cost risk (item 3). A StoreKit subscription with App Attest funds the AI and locks the proxy to genuine copies of the app.
4. **Show changes before committing an AI plan.** Show what changes, flag anything over a sensible jump, and allow undo. That builds trust in the AI coach and removes the crash risk.
5. **Add kg support.** It was deferred in `UPDATE_PLAN.md`, but most of the world lifts in kg. Plate math, increments and the watch all need it, so plan it before an international launch.
